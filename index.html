02 / The studio

Small studio.
Serious attention.

PISMARK is the independent practice of Youssef Hamdy, a frontend developer and digital designer based in Cairo. I partner with ambitious founders and brands to make their digital presence feel as considered as their product.

The work is equal parts taste and technical care: purposeful strategy, distinctive design, and clean builds that move quickly and scale gracefully.
